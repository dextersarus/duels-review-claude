# Princess Aggro vs Circle of Life (Amber/Emerald mirror) — notes from simulation and replay review

- Princess wins about 66% against strong play. Going first: 81%. Going second: 51%. Always choose to go first.
- Circle sweeps in about 40% of games; Princess wins 49% of those vs 78% without a sweep.
- Circle of Life resolving drops Princess to about 25%.
- Elinor triggers matter: 2+ triggers is about 78%. Plan turns so 3+ characters are exerted at end of turn.
- With Ursula - Deceiver, take Circle of Life first; otherwise take tempo (Akood et Emuti) or the missing half of their sweep. Check for second copies in the revealed hand.
- Against Max Goof, songs discarded by Ursula come back (Rebellious Teen returns cost 3 or less; Chart Topper replays cost 4 or less).
- When Prince Phillip is in their discard, count their Sing Together total from every singer that can exert next turn (exerted ones ready). Remove a singer to stay under 8 (Finnick, challenges, Elinor pings). Also check hard-casting with ink drops.
- Dale - Ready for His Shot: exerted singers are free kills with Spike Suit.
- Mickey Mouse - Best in Town gives the opponent ink drops too; check their 9-drop math.
- The strong Circle player pays ink for Malicious, Mean, and Scary when no singer is ready, so an empty Circle board does not mean no sweep.
