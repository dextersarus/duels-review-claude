# Amber/Emerald Princess Aggro: playing guide

Written by a strong player on our team. Use it when the player you're reviewing (`REVIEW FROM:`) is on Amber/Emerald Princess Aggro. Treat it as expert heuristics, not rules: the board state, the card text in the transcript's card reference and `review-rules.md` always win. When a play follows or breaks this guide, say so and judge whether the guide was right for that position.

Names used below:
- **Amber Champion** is **Mickey Mouse - Amber Champion**.
- **Big booties** isn't one card: it means any character with a lot of willpower. They're the ones that pair well with Dale's ability.
- **BBL** also just means a lot of willpower: **David** and **Thomas** are 1-drops with 4 willpower each, so they survive early challenges and keep questing.
- The rest (Aurora, Pluto, Willow, Little Pokie, Thomas, David, Elinor, Dale, Ursula, Mowgli) are short names: match each to the full card name in the replay's card reference before relying on a tip, and never guess a card's text from its name. If a name doesn't match any card in the deck, ignore the tips that need it.

## How the deck wins
Go wide fast with cheap characters and cost reducers, then cash in with 4-drops. Aim to have 4 characters on the board on turn 2. Elinor rewards ending the turn with 3 or more of your characters exerted.

## Mulligan: what a keep looks like
Keep (or mulligan toward) a hand with all of:
- At least one cost-reducing 1-drop: **Aurora** or **Pluto**.
- One or two (no more) cost-reducing 2-drops ("ramp"): **Willow** or **Little Pokie**. If you can only have one, Willow is the best.
- At least one other 1-drop.
- Any 4-cost character: **Elinor** on the play, **Dale** on the draw, **Amber Champion** against Steel.
What doesn't fit goes to the bottom.

**On the play, the ideal opener:**
- Pluto or Aurora.
- Willow or Little Pokie. With Willow you also want a second Willow or a Little Pokie, depending on the turn-1 reducer: with Pluto either is great; with Aurora it has to be Little Pokie.
- A 1-drop, usually **Thomas**: with 4 willpower he's hard to challenge early, so he'll usually get at least 2 quests in.
- A 4-drop, ideally Elinor. Against Steel, Amber Champion over anything else.
"Pluto, Willow, Thomas" plus a 4-drop is a great hand.

**On the draw:** the same, but push even harder for Dale and/or Amber Champion. Against song-heavy decks, keeping **Ursula** or **Mowgli** for a turn-1 1-drop, turn-2 Ursula/Mowgli line (then dumping the rest on turn 3) may be right. *The author marks this as untested: mention it as an option, don't call it a mistake either way.*

## Turn-3 4-drop order
| | On the play | On the draw |
|---|---|---|
| vs Steel | Amber Champion, then Elinor (slightly) over Dale | Amber Champion, Dale, Elinor. Amber Champion into Steel is essential. |
| vs anything else or unknown | Elinor, Amber Champion, Dale | Dale, Elinor, Amber Champion |

With **Amber Champion and Dale** both in hand, play Amber Champion first: he survives Thunderbolt and makes Dale survive it too once Dale lands the next turn. The author hasn't found an exception.

## Ideal starts
- T1 Pluto. T2 Willow, exert Pluto, David exerted. T3 Dale, Amber Champion or Elinor.
- T1 Pluto. T2 Willow, exert Pluto, a second Willow (Willow and Pluto discounts), then a 1-drop from the second Willow's discount. T3 a 4-drop.
- T1 Aurora. T2 Willow, quest with Aurora, Little Pokie and a 1-drop. T3 a 4-drop.
- T1 Pluto or Aurora. T2 Little Pokie and a 1-drop, exert Pluto for another 1-drop (or Aurora for more Auroras).

If there's any way to get 4 characters down on turn 2, do it. Even better: bait the opponent into challenging a 1-drop reducer so you can punish their character. Favourite line: T1 Aurora; T2 Willow, Little Pokie and a 1-drop; the opponent's 2/2 challenges Aurora and takes a damage; T3 quest with everything, play Elinor, and at end of turn ping off the damaged 2/2, draw a card and gain a lore.

## Main combos
- 3 or more characters plus Elinor.
- Big booties (characters with lots of willpower) plus Dale.
- Dale plus Amber Champion.
